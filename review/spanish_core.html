Spanish core — card reader

How to review

Tap a card to flip it and read the English side: the translation, the example translation and the notes.

Flag whatever reads wrong in the English with the Translation / Sentence / Notes / Other buttons, and write a note whenever a flag needs words. Long-press any field on the back (or tap its ✏️) to mark the exact words that need changing — then rewrite them yourself, or leave the mark for the computer.

When you are done, open Export report, copy it or mail it, and send the report back. Everything you mark is kept on this phone until then.
