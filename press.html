What FlashBoss is

FlashBoss is a desktop terminal application for vocabulary acquisition. A spaced-repetition scheduler deals cards; each card carries a target word, a constructed example sentence built only from vocabulary already introduced, translations, optional study notes, and text-to-speech on the target-language fields. Cards are grouped into themed clusters, and no cluster leaves rotation until it is beaten in a boss fight.

The boss fight is the graduation mechanic and the part worth filming. Learner and boss share one linear track: a correct answer advances the learner a step and shoves the boss a step toward the far edge, a wrong answer slides the learner two steps back. Positions 1 and 2 deal the cluster's easiest cards; positions 3 and 4 deal its hardest — so a cluster cannot be conquered until its weakest words are answered.

Six masters set the daily pace, from 8 cards a day to 34 — roughly five to thirty minutes. Ratings move cards along a Fibonacci interval sequence, and a cluster becomes eligible for its fight once 80% of its cards reach the 13-day interval. The full mechanical reference, with citations, is on the How FlashBoss Teaches page.

Interface and audio

Five interface languages

The whole game — menus, lessons, boss fights, achievements, the masters' own dialogue — runs in English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Simplified Chinese. The first launch asks which; Ctrl+L switches at any moment, from any screen. Interface language and card language are chosen independently, so the German course can be studied through Japanese while the menus stay in Spanish.

English text is American by default, with a per-hero British spelling layer covering cards, lessons, cluster names and answer checking.

Text-to-speech

Audio is generated by the neural text-to-speech engine the game ships with (piper1-gpl, GPL v3; the corresponding source is included with the game). It is not recorded human voice, and it is not locked to the cards: Ctrl+P opens a pronounce window that will speak or save any text you paste into it, in any installed language.

Accessibility — please report these accurately

  • Type size is not adjustable in-game; it follows the terminal.
  • The game does not support screen readers.
  • The pronounce window is mouse-driven.
  • Windows 10 needs Windows Terminal (a free Microsoft Store download). A numeric keypad is recommended on every platform.

Short description (approx. 40 words)

FlashBoss is a terminal vocabulary trainer: spaced repetition, example sentences built only from words you already know, text-to-speech on every target word, and a boss fight at the end of every cluster. Five packs ship free. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Boilerplate (approx. 90 words)

FlashBoss is a desktop vocabulary trainer that hides a serious spaced-repetition course inside RPG armour. Cards arrive in themed clusters, each target word set in a sentence built only from vocabulary already taught, spoken aloud by the game's neural text-to-speech engine. No cluster leaves rotation until you beat its boss — and the fight deals your weakest words first. Twenty-three packs are out, from full 3,000-word courses in German, Spanish, Italian, Esperanto and French to English etymology through its Germanic, Norman, Latin and Greek roots. It runs in English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

Developer boilerplate

FlashBoss is made by Odiin, a solo developer based in Germany, who built it out of his own frustration at knowing thousands of German words he could not use with confidence. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux via Steam.

Who it is for

Self-directed language learners; Anki users who want structure, a syllabus and a reason to come back; readers building English vocabulary for study or work; and anyone who likes a terminal and a low-distraction study tool that runs on almost any machine.

What makes it different

Every example sentence is constructed, not scraped, so the target word is usually the only unknown on the card. Progress is gated: a cluster is conquered by defeating a boss with your hardest cards, not by clicking through a queue. And the whole thing runs in five languages, so the course does not have to be studied through English.

Press and affiliate FAQ

Can I get a review key?

Yes. Email flashbosscontact@gmail.com with a link to your channel, publication or platform.

Is there a trailer?

Yes, on the Steam page. Ask if you need the raw file.

Can my readers try it before buying?

The boss-fight demo is playable in any browser, and the full word lists and lessons for every pack are published on this site. Five packs are also free inside the base game.

How do I promote it as an affiliate?

Register a Steam UTM link and you are paid a flat $0.50 (USD) on every sale Steam attributes to it. Full terms, thresholds and payout timing are on the promote page.

Is it a subscription?

No. The base game is bought once and the packs are bought once. There are no accounts and no recurring charges.

Does it teach speaking?

No, and the developer says so on the record. FlashBoss trains reading, listening and recognition — the input half. Speaking and writing practice is left to conversation classes, tutors and other tools.

Is the audio recorded by native speakers?

No. All audio is text-to-speech, generated by the neural engine bundled with the game. Please do not describe it as native or recorded voice.
