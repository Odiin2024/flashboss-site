Voices

What it sounds like

FlashBoss pronounces every card, and the built-in PiperTTS window (open with Ctrl + P in-game) allows you to type or paste any text for pronunciation in your target language. Scroll down to see examples for each voice.

Voice DLC, sold on its own

a different accent, everywhere

A voice DLC replaces the included voice across every pack in that language — cards, lessons, boss fights, the lot. Switch back to the built-in voice whenever you like; nothing is lost. Each card below also plays the voice it replaces, saying the same sentence, so you can hear exactly what changes before you spend anything.

The PiperTTS window — read anything

Press Ctrl + P anywhere in the game and a pronunciation window opens. Paste in a word, a sentence, a paragraph of something you are reading — it speaks it in the voice you are learning with, and saves it to a file if you want to keep it. It works while a card is on screen, in the menus, in the middle of a boss fight.
