TOMMI Games Award 2026 — nominated

"Flash Boss" wraps language learning in a surprisingly entertaining role-playing setting and combines classic learning content with dungeon-style visuals, game-like progression, and humorous elements. Whether it's English, Spanish, or Latin, exercises and language challenges become part of the user's own adventure, creating a motivating framework for learning.

— from the jury's write-up, TOMMI Games Award 2026
