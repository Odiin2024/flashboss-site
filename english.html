English Core design

The first thousand words

Five tiers, forty clusters of 25 cards, each cluster gated by a boss fight.

English Core is the highest-frequency thousand, walked in order: the engine verbs and little words that run every English sentence, then people and home, the town and the working day, and by the fifth tier the language of law, state, and the wider world. The example sentences grow with you and never run ahead of what you've been taught.

How it sticks

Flashcards as an integrated system

Fibonacci SRS
Rate each card 0–5. The better you know a word, the longer before it returns — spaced repetition on Fibonacci intervals.
Boss fights
Each of the forty clusters is gated by a duel you can't win without confronting and overcoming your most difficult words.
Graduation
Beat a cluster and its cards leave your daily deck for good. The deck gets smaller as you learn.
Your language
Translation, translated example, and study notes in German, Spanish, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese on every card — and the interface speaks all four, plus English.
Audio
Clean English Piper text-to-speech on every word and example sentence. It is synthesis, not a recording of a speaker — and we say so.
Two spellings
American English base with a full British spelling option — colour or color, at the flick of a setting.
Lessons
38 reference lessons — word order, articles, tenses — each firing at the card where you first need it, readable in the learner's language.
Frequency
Words arrive by how often English actually uses them: the thousand that carry the most language come first.

Questions

The things people ask before they buy.

I'm a native English speaker — should I buy this?

No. It teaches the first thousands of English words — words you already know. Your English packs are Adept and Advance, free inside the base game, and the Roots Series teaches you where your language came from.

Which languages can I learn English from?

German, Spanish, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Every card carries its translation, translated example sentence, and study notes in all four, and the game interface speaks each of them. No English is needed to play.

Do I need anything else to play it?

Yes. English Core is a DLC for the FlashBoss base game, so you need the base game as well. Pareto 1 requires Core, and Pareto 2 requires Pareto 1 — the packs build on each other in order. On Windows 10 you also need Windows Terminal — a free, secure download from the Microsoft Store.

Can I see the word lists before I buy?

Yes — all three. Core, Pareto 1 and Pareto 2 are on the word lists page, free to read before you buy. There is also a playable boss-fight demo in the browser: try the demo.
