In Development

What is being built, and how far along it is. Nothing on this page has a release date, because none of it has one — a date goes up when a pack is ready, not before. Everything listed here exists as real cards today; the counts were taken from the packs themselves, not from a plan.

▍ Cards written — the pack is authored and counted. What remains is checking, audio and a store page.
▍ Planned — decided and inventoried, not started. No cards exist yet.

Finishing a course already on sale

These continue packs you can buy today, and take the pack before them as read.

Swahili Pareto 1Cards written
1,000 words · 40 clusters · tiers 6–10

The working vocabulary: money and the bank, the company and the contract, the ministry, elections, the court, the police, the press, the hospital. Continues Swahili Core, which is out now.

Swahili Pareto 2Cards written
1,000 words · 40 clusters · tiers 11–15

The last thousand of the 3,000-word course, and where Swahili's derivation opens up — one root becoming six verbs through the passive, the stative, the reciprocal, the reflexive, the causative and the applicative.

The Guide Part IICards written
925 cards · 50 clusters · three tiers · English and German

The other half of a game master's memory. Where The Guide holds the rules you adjudicate with, Part II holds what you populate a world with: the spell list, the creature roster and the treasure table. Requires The Guide.

New languages

Each is a full course in the usual shape — Core first, then Pareto 1, five tiers apiece.

RussianCards written
Core and Pareto 1 · 1,000 words each · 40 clusters each

Русский. Two thousand words authored and counted.

IndonesianCards written
Core and Pareto 1 · 1,000 words each · 40 clusters each

Bahasa Indonesia — a language with no tenses, no genders and no plurals to memorise, and a word order you already have.

Modern GreekCards written
Core and Pareto 1 · 1,000 words each · 40 clusters each

Ελληνικά, the living language, with an alphabet ladder in the opening lessons. Not to be confused with Ancient Greek below, or with Greek Roots, which teaches the Greek already inside English.

Ancient GreekCards written
Core and Pareto 1 · 1,000 words each · 40 clusters each

A reading course, in the shape Latin took: the sentences start built for the tier and end as the authors wrote them.

Polish and PortuguesePlanned
Word lists locked · no cards authored yet

The frequency backbones are chosen and fixed, which is the half of the work that decides what a course teaches. The cards themselves are not written.

Beyond the Latin trunk

Latin Core and Latin Pareto are the whole 2,000-word reading course, grammar-complete at tier 10. There is deliberately no third staircase.

Latin — the two branchesPlanned
Classical poetry · or ecclesiastical Latin

Past 2,000 words Latin genuinely divides, and which thousand comes next depends on what you want to read: classical poetry — Vergil, Ovid, Catullus, Horace — or ecclesiastical Latin, the Vulgate, the hymns and the liturgy. Both are inventoried and reserved. Neither is built, and neither takes the other as a prerequisite.

Voices

Castellano — Spain, maleCards written
Replaces the included Latin American voice across every Spanish pack

A voice pack rather than a course: it swaps the voice in cards, lessons and boss fights, and switches back whenever you like. Hear it against the voice it replaces on the voices page.
