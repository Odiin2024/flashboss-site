## Things that were wrong, and are now not

These are the ones worth your eye, because they were live.

**1. Every course carries Russian, and the site said it did not.** I counted the
`Translation_*` keys on every card of every released pack. Toki Pona, French, English as
a foreign language, Italian, Esperanto, Latin, Swahili — all carry de, es, ja, **ru** and
zh on every card, and every tier of lessons has a Russian twin. The interface is six:
`i18n.py:45` reads `("en", "de", "ja", "es", "zh", "ru")`.

The site said four or five in about thirty places across six locales. Fixed in all of
them, including the card samples, which now show five languages and have gained their
Russian row from the pack's own fields.

**The sharp one:** `english.ru.html` told Russian readers, in Russian,
*"русского среди них нет"* — Russian is not among them. It is. That sentence and four
others on that page are corrected.

This is the fourth or fifth time this count has drifted. I found it with the
sentence-level scan rather than a keyword grep, exactly as your note says to.

**2. The Roots cluster counts were stale in all six locales.** German said 48 (it is 47),
Norman 49 (48), Greek 55 (56), and the uncommitted 09-13 edit had Latin Roots at 50 (30).
Corrected everywhere, three places per page. knight-e6 confirmed the four counts are
final before the depots go live.

**3. `latin.html` claimed three card languages.** It said German, Spanish and Japanese.
Reading the cluster JSON says de, es, ja, ru, zh plus the `_gb` layer — five, not three,
and 30,000 fields rather than 24,000. Its lesson counts were stale too: Core carries 26
reference lessons not 22, Pareto 18 not 17, **44 in total, not 39**.

**4. `press.html` was a month out of date** — the page a journalist reads. 23 packs and
21,500 cards became **29 packs and 27,705 cards**, counted pack by pack. English Pareto 1
and 2, Latin, Swahili and The Guide joined the tables. Adept's "roughly C1" is gone, since
CEFR is banned on the immersion surfaces. The Castilian voice is marked not yet released,
because it is not in the live DLC list.

**5. The Russian lessons page was showing English lessons.** Russian lesson twins exist
upstream for every pack and always have; `sync_lessons.py` never listed `_ru` among the
locales it copies, and `lessons.ru.html` was a generation behind and had no `LESSON_LOC`
at all, so it never asked for one. Both fixed. The six foreign courses were not in that
sync map either — their lesson files were hand-dropped long ago and nothing had refreshed
them, so that refresh is most of a 410-file diff and it is upstream fixing real content:
the German Esperanto lessons still carried untranslated English body text.

**6. Localized lesson titles printed themselves twice** — "Урок 1: УРОК 1: …", "Lektion
61: LEKTION 61: …". The title stripper only knew the English form. 439 of 444 Russian
titles carry that prefix, and comparable counts in the other four locales, so it was on
every localized lesson on the site.

**7. Things that shipped weeks ago were still announced as coming.** A sweep for
future-tense language checked against the live DLC list found four:

  - The **Japanese home banner** said English Core and French Pareto 1 were *in
    production* and Latin *in preparation* — all three shipped in August or early
    September. The **Chinese one** promised Spanish and Italian were *coming soon*;
    Spanish has been out since March, Italian since April. Both now say the true and
    stronger thing: every course carries their language on every card and lesson.
  - **English Pareto 1 and 2** were drawn as locked grey panels reading "next in
    sequence" in all six locales. Both released 31 August. They are ordinary linked
    cards now.
  - **english.ru.html** marked English Core "без русского интерфейса" and locked it —
    the last survivor of the Russian claim, on the card a Russian reader would click.
  - **The about pages** said "with Latin in production" in all six languages.
  - **The voices page** called the Latin voice "coming soon" and said it *will* come
    with packs that shipped a fortnight ago, while the Castilian voice — which is
    genuinely not released — was presented as buyable. Both corrected, six locales each.

**8. The word lists were throwing away your cluster names.** Every pack ships
`cluster_names.json` and a file per locale, authored and translated and checked, and the
site built every label from the folder name instead — so Greek Roots' first cluster read
"Famous Twenty" in all six locales where the authored name is "It's all Greek to me", and
the German page should have said "Das kommt mir griechisch vor". That is change (a) of
`ROOTS_WORDLIST_PROPOSAL_2026-09-21`, which your own proposal calls a bug fix. Taken on
its own commit so it reverts on its own. **Change (b), the etymology line, is untouched
and still waiting on your ruling** — it is the expensive one.
